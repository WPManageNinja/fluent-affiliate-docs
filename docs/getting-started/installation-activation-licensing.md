# Installation, Activation & Licensing

Installing and activating FluentAffiliate is a quick and easy process. This guide will walk you through the steps to install FluentAffiliate and activate your license so you can start managing your affiliate program right away.

## Step 1. Install FluentAffiliate (Free Version)

This is the standard method for installing the core FluentAffiliate plugin, which is free and available from the WordPress Plugin Directory. Follow the steps below:

1. Navigate to your WordPress dashboard.
2. Go to **Plugins** > **Add New**.
3. In the search bar, type "**FluentAffiliate**" and press **Enter**.
4. Locate the "**FluentAffiliate**" plugin in the search results and click "**Install Now**".
5. Once installed, click "**Activate**". You can now use the **FluentAffiliate** plugin.


## Step 2. Installing FluentAffiliate Pro (Requires Base Plugin)

If you have purchased FluentAffiliate Pro for advanced features, you will need to upload and activate them. Ensure the Free Base Plugin (from step 1) is already installed and active before proceeding with the Pro plugin.

1. Download the FluentAffiliate Pro **.zip** file from your [WPManageNinja dashboard](https://wpmanageninja.com/account/).
2. Navigate to your WordPress dashboard.
3. Go to **Plugins** > **Add New**.
4. At the top of the page, click the "**Upload Plugin**" button.
5. Click "**Choose File**", then select the **FluentAffiliate Pro .zip** file you downloaded.
6. Click "**Install Now**".
7. Once the installation is complete, click "**Activate Plugin**".


## Step 3. Activating Your FluentAffiliate Pro License

After installing the Pro, you will need to activate your license key. This is essential to receive automatic updates, bug fixes, new features, and access to our support for FluentAffiliate Pro.

1. When you activate the Pro plugin, you might see an activation notice directly in your WordPress dashboard. You can click on this notice to start the activation process.
2. Alternatively, go to the **FluentAffiliate** (shown as **FluentAffiliate Pro** once Pro is active) menu in your WordPress admin sidebar.
3. Click **Settings**.
4. In the settings menu on the left, click **License Management**.
5. In the "**License Key**" field, enter the unique license key you received upon purchasing **FluentAffiliate Pro** from **WPManageNinja**.
6. Click the "**Verify License**" button.

Once your license is verified, the **License Management** page shows your current **plan** and **expiry date**. You will also receive a renewal reminder when your license is close to expiring.

Each plugin update is verified before installation to make sure the update comes from [WPManageNinja](https://wpmanageninja.com/account/) and that the files have not been changed or tampered with.

After activation, the setup wizard opens. Continue with the [Onboarding & Setup](/guide/getting-started/onboarding-setup) guide.

That's it! You've successfully installed, activated, and licensed FluentAffiliate.
