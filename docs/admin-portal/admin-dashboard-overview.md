# Admin Dashboard Overview

FluentAffiliate makes it easy to manage all your affiliate activity right from your WordPress dashboard. As the admin, you'll get a clear and simple view of how your affiliate program is performing in your FluentAffiliate Admin dashboard.

From this dashboard, you'll see key stats like [payouts](/guide/admin-portal/payout-management), earnings, [referrals](/guide/admin-portal/managing-referrals), visits, and more, all in one place. It helps you stay on top of what your [affiliates](/guide/admin-portal/managing-affiliates) are doing and how they're performing.

## What You'll See on the Dashboard

Let's take a look at the different sections of the dashboard and what they tell you.

## Key Performance Metrics

At the top of the dashboard, you'll find a series of boxes showing your most important stats:

* **Total Paid:** The total amount already paid out to your affiliates.
* **Total Unpaid:** The total amount of approved commissions that are waiting to be paid, including referrals in a payout that is still being processed.
* **Active Affiliates:** The number of approved affiliates in your program.
* **Total Visits:** The number of visits their [affiliate links](/guide/affiliate-portal/generating-affiliate-links) have received.
* **Total Referrals:** How many referrals they've successfully brought in.
* **Conversion Rate:** The percentage of visits that have turned into successful referrals.
* **Pending Affiliates:** The number of new affiliate applications that are awaiting your approval.
* **Total Order Value by Referrals:** The total revenue amount generated from all affiliate referrals.

### Dark Mode / Light Mode Toggle
In the top navbar, on every FluentAffiliate admin page, next to the **Settings** button, you'll find the display mode toggle. Simply click the **moon** icon to switch the entire interface to a **dark** theme, which can be easier on the eyes in low-light environments. To switch back, click the **sun** icon that appears in its place.

### Growth Overview

Below the main stats, the Growth Overview graph gives you a visual representation of your program's performance over time.

* The graph plots **Referrals**, **Visits**, and new **Affiliates**, with a legend to tell the lines apart.

* Use the **date range picker** at the top right of the graph to view data for specific periods. It shows the last 6 months by default.

### Right Sidebar

The sidebar on the right gives you a real-time snapshot of recent activity. It has two cards, showing 5 entries each.

* **Recent Referrals:** This list shows the latest 5 referrals made through your affiliate links. You can quickly see the description, the commission amount, the affiliate responsible, and a status badge (e.g., Unpaid, Pending, or Paid). For detailed referral tracking and management, visit the [Managing Referrals](/guide/admin-portal/managing-referrals) section.

* **Top Affiliates:** This is your affiliate leaderboard. It highlights your top 5 active affiliates by total earnings, showing their referrals and earnings, making it easy to see who your most valuable partners are. You can click on any affiliate to view their detailed [profile](/guide/miscellaneous/affiliate-profile).
