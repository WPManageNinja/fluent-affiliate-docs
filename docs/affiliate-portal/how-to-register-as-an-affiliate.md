# How to Register as an Affiliate

Becoming an affiliate on your site using **Fluent Affiliate** is easy. Whether someone is a long time promoter or just starting their journey, the registration process is quick, simple, and designed for ease.

Let's walk through how a user can register and get started as an affiliate.

## Step 1: Access the Affiliate Dashboard

When a user visits your site's **Affiliate Page** usually linked in the menu or footer, they'll see a simple, user friendly interface. From here, they can log in to their affiliate dashboard or click the **Register** button to sign up and become an affiliate.

Click the **Register Now** button on the login screen to start the sign-up process.

>[!NOTE]
>Only the site admin can decide where the **Affiliate Page** appears on the site. To learn how to set this up, check out this [article](/guide/settings-and-customization/referral-settings).


![Affiliate Registration Page](/images/affiliate-portal/registration/registration-page.webp)

>[!NOTE]
>If your site uses a security plugin that shows an extra challenge before login, the affiliate login form sends the user to the standard WordPress login page to complete it, and then returns them to the affiliate portal page they came from. This also works on sites with plain permalinks.

## Step 2: Fill Out the Registration Form

After clicking **Register Now**, users will land on the Affiliate Registration page. Here, they'll be asked to provide the necessary details to create their affiliate account. It is divided into clear sections for ease of use. The order below is the default one; the site admin can reorder or hide some fields, and with FluentAffiliate Pro can add custom fields and a captcha.

**Account Information**

This section covers the basic details needed to create the account:

* **Full name**
* **Email Address**
* **Username**
* **Account Password**
* **Re-type Account Password**

**About Yourself**

This part of the form helps the site owner understand more about their potential partners:

* **How will you promote us?:** A field to describe marketing methods and strategies. 
* **Website URL:** The user's blog or website link, which helps verify their online presence. 

**Final Agreement and Submission**

To complete the process, the user must:

1. Check the box to confirm they "**I agree to the terms and conditions.**" 

2. Fill in the **Payment Email Address** (the affiliate's PayPal email where commissions are sent). If the site pays by bank transfer, this field is **Bank Details** instead.

3. Click the **Register** button to submit the form.


![Registration Form](/images/affiliate-portal/registration/registration-form.webp)

## Step 3: Welcome to the Affiliate Dashboard

After the user submits the form, an email with a **verification code** is sent to them (valid for 10 minutes). This step is on by default. They enter the code to confirm their email address.

If the site requires admin approval for new affiliates, the user sees an "application is in review" message until the **site admin** approves it. Otherwise the account is active right away. Once active, they can log in. Upon logging in, they will be directed to their personal **Affiliate Dashboard**.

This dashboard is their central hub to:

* Track performance, including visits, referrals, and earnings.

* Generate unique affiliate links for promoting products.

* View their history of payouts.

* Update their account settings.

To learn more about the Affiliate Dashboard, check out this [article](/guide/affiliate-portal/affiliate-dashboard-overview).

![Affiliate Dashboard](/images/affiliate-portal/registration/registration-success.webp)

::: tip For site owners
You can also place the registration form on any page with the `[fluent_affiliate_register_form]` shortcode.
:::

And that's it! In just a few simple steps, a new user can join the affiliate program and get everything they need to start promoting and earning.
