# Key Features Overview

FluentAffiliate isn't just another affiliate plugin, it's the smoothest way to turn your website into a full-fledged affiliate marketing machine. Whether you're running a WooCommerce, EDD, SureCart store, or selling courses with an LMS, FluentAffiliate helps you grow through referral marketing. Let's take a quick tour of the features that make it stand out.

## Admin Portal

Your own **control** for managing the entire affiliate ecosystem.

* **Admin Dashboard** – View top affiliates, recent referrals, payout summaries, and more in one glance.
* **Affiliate Management** – Approve, reject, or review affiliates. View their activity in one place.
* **Referral Management** – View all the referral details.
* **Payout Management** – Generate one-time or bulk payouts.
* **Multi-Domain Support** – Running more than one site? Manage referrals from multiple domains with ease.

## Affiliate Portal

This is the **center** for your affiliates. Once they sign up, they'll get their own dashboard to manage everything.

* **Affiliate Dashboard** – Clean, insightful view of earnings, referrals, visits, and more.
* **Affiliate Registration** – Built-in form to let users sign up as affiliates.
* **Affiliate Links** – Auto-generated unique referral links.
* **Referral Tracking** – Real-time tracking of referrals with status updates.
* **Payout History** – Affiliates can see how much they've earned and when they got paid.
* **Visit History** – Want to know how many clicks an affiliate is driving? It's all here.
* **Settings Panel** – Affiliates can update their payment info and choose what notifications to receive.

## Settings

Customize the engine to match your strategy.

* **Referral Settings** – Set how much commission affiliates get, whether to credit first or last referrer, and more.
* **Affiliate Groups** – Segment affiliates into groups for custom rules or reporting.
* **General Settings** – Define affiliate area page, terms of use, and default settings.
* **Email Settings** – Customize every part of the emails sent to affiliates.
* **Integration Settings** – Seamless plug-and-play with popular plugins like:
  * WooCommerce
  * Easy Digital Downloads
  * FluentCRM
  * Fluent Forms
  * Fluent Booking
  * MemberPress
  * LifterLMS
  * LearnDash
  * FluentCart
  * EasyCart
  * SureCart
  * GiveWP
* **One Click Migration** – Coming from another affiliate plugin like AffiliateWP? Migrate your data in a snap.

Whether you're a solopreneur or running a big online platform, FluentAffiliate scales with you. No complex setup, no bloated tools — just smart features that work out of the box.
