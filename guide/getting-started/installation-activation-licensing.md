# Installation, Activation & Licensing

Installing and activating FluentAffiliate is a quick and easy process. This guide will walk you through the steps to install, upgrade, and activate your FluentAffiliate license so you can start managing your affiliate program right away.

### Step 1: Install FluentAffiliate

First, download the plugin from your FluentAffiliate account dashboard. After downloading the plugin, follow the steps below:

1. Go to your WordPress Dashboard.
2. Navigate to **Plugins → Add New**.
3. Click on **Upload Plugin**.
4. Upload the FluentAffiliate `.zip` file you downloaded.
5. Click **Install Now**, then **Activate** the plugin.

### Step 2: Activate the License

To activate your FluentAffiliate license and receive updates and support:

1. Go to **FluentAffiliate → Settings**.
2. Navigate to the **License** tab.
3. Enter your **license key** (you'll find it in your account dashboard under the Licenses section).
4. Click on the **Activate License** button.

Once activated, you'll get access to plugin updates and premium support.

### Step 3: Keep FluentAffiliate Updated

Keeping your plugin updated ensures access to new features, improvements, and bug fixes. If your license is activated, you'll get update notifications directly in your WordPress dashboard.

To update:

1. Go to **Dashboard → Updates**.
2. Check for the FluentAffiliate update.
3. Click **Update Now**.

That's it! You've successfully installed, activated, and licensed FluentAffiliate.
