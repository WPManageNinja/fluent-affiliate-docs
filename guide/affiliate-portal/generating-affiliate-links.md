# Generating Affiliate Links

Want to earn from promoting products on the site? As an affiliate, you can easily create your own unique referral links in just a few simple steps.

In this guide, we'll walk you through how to generate and manage affiliate links right from your dashboard.

### Step 1: Go to the Links Page

First, log in to your affiliate account dashboard. From the top menu, click on **Links.** This will take you to the **Referrals URL Generator** page.

**Referral URL Generator**: Use this field to create a custom referral link for the site.

**Your Referral Link**: This is your default affiliate link provided by the site.

![Referral URL Generator](/guide/public/images/affiliate-portal/generating-links/link-generator-form.webp)

### Step 2: Add the Product or Page URL

Now, find the product or page you want to promote. Copy that URL and paste it into the **Referral URL Generator** field.

As soon as you paste the link, FluentAffiliate will automatically generate your unique referral link right below.

Once your referral link appears, just click on the **link** or the **Copy** button next to it to copy the link.

![Generated Referral Link](/guide/public/images/affiliate-portal/generating-links/generated-affiliate-link.webp)

And that's it! You've successfully created a referral link.
