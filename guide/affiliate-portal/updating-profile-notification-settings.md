# Updating Profile & Notification Settings

Inside Fluent Affiliate, managing your basic account settings is simple and quick. Whether you want to update your PayPal email for payouts or control your email notifications. Let's see how it works.

## Settings

Start by logging into your affiliate account. Once you're in, you'll land on the dashboard where you can track your performance.

From the top menu, click on the **Settings** tab. This section allows you to manage two important settings related to your affiliate account.

### Add or Update Your PayPal Email

The first setting you'll see is the **PayPal Email** field.

This is the email address where you can receive your affiliate payouts. If you haven't added one yet, simply type in your PayPal email. Already added it, but need to change? Just update the field with your new email.

### Manage Email Notifications

Below the PayPal email field, you'll find the **Email Notification** setting.

Here, you'll see a checkbox:  
**Enable New Referral Email Notification** – If this box is checked, you'll get an email every time someone uses your affiliate link and a new referral is recorded.

If you prefer not to receive these updates, just uncheck the box.

![User Portal Settings](/guide/public/images/affiliate-portal/profile-settings/profile-settings-page.webp)

Once you're done, hit the **Save** button to apply your updates.
