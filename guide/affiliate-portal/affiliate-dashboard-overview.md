# Affiliate Dashboard Overview

The Affiliate Dashboard is your central hub for managing your affiliate activities. This guide will walk you through all the features and sections available in your dashboard.

## Accessing the Dashboard

To access your Affiliate Dashboard:

1. Log in to your WordPress site
2. Navigate to the Affiliate Portal
3. You'll be taken to your dashboard automatically

Here's what your dashboard looks like:

![Affiliate Dashboard Overview](/guide/public/images/affiliate-portal/dashboard/affiliate-dashboard-overview.webp)

## Dashboard Sections

### 1. Performance Overview

At the top of your dashboard, you'll find key performance metrics:

- Total Earnings
- Pending Commissions
- Paid Commissions
- Total Referrals
- Conversion Rate
- Click-through Rate

### 2. Recent Activity

Below the performance metrics, you'll see your recent activity:

- Latest referrals
- Recent commissions
- Recent payouts
- Latest clicks

### 3. Quick Actions

On the right side of your dashboard, you'll find quick action buttons:

- Generate Affiliate Link
- View Reports
- Update Profile
- Access Support

### 4. Earnings Graph

The earnings graph shows your commission trends over time:

- Daily earnings
- Monthly earnings
- Yearly earnings
- Custom date range

### 5. Navigation Menu

The left sidebar contains links to all major sections:

- Dashboard Home
- Affiliate Links
- Referrals
- Payouts
- Reports
- Settings

## Tips for Using Your Dashboard

1. **Check Daily**: Monitor your performance metrics daily to track progress
2. **Generate Links**: Use the quick link generator for new promotions
3. **Track Payments**: Keep an eye on pending and processed commissions
4. **Update Profile**: Keep your payment and contact information current

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

Here are solutions to common dashboard issues:

- **Stats Not Updating**: Try refreshing the page or clearing your browser cache
- **Missing Data**: Ensure all your tracking links are properly formatted
- **Access Problems**: Verify your affiliate account is active and approved
