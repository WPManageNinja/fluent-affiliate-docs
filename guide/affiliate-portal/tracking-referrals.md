# Tracking Referrals

Once an affiliate logs into their account, they can easily track how well their promotions are performing right from the referrals. The **Referrals** tab is where they'll find all the details about the referrals' purchases using their unique affiliate links.

In this guide, we'll walk you through how affiliate users can view and understand their referrals in FluentAffiliate.

## Referrals

Affiliates need to log in to their account through the FluentAffiliate **User Portal**. Once logged in, they can click on the **Referrals** tab from the top menu to view all the referral details linked to their account.

Here's what each column in the referral table means:

* **Date**: The exact date and time when the referral took place.
* **Description**: Shows which product or action triggered the referral and earned the affiliate a commission.
* **Amount**: Displays how much commission was earned from that referral.
* **Status**: Tells whether the referral is **Paid**, **Unpaid**, or **Rejected**.

![Referrals User Portal](/guide/public/images/affiliate-portal/referrals/referrals-list-view.webp)

All referrals are listed in a table format, making it super simple to scroll through and analyze.

Knowing which promotions are bringing in referrals helps affiliates fine tune their strategy. They can see what's working, track progress, and plan their next steps to increase conversions.
