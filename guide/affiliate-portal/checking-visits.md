# Checking Visits

The Visits section helps you track all the clicks you receive on your affiliate links. It’s a great way to see if your promotional efforts are successfully driving traffic to the website.

To check your visits, log in to your affiliate portal and click on the **Visits** tab in the main navigation.

## Referral URL Visits (Unique)

This table provides a clean, simple log of the unique clicks your affiliate links have received. Each row represents a single visit and contains the following information:

* **Date:** The exact date and time the visitor clicked your link.
* **URL:** The specific affiliate link that was clicked.
* **Refer:** This shows where the click came from.

This section gives affiliates a complete overview of their link activity and helps them understand which channels or content are performing best.
