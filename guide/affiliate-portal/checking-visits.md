# Checking Visits

The Visits section in the FluentAffiliate User Portal helps affiliates track all the clicks they receive on their affiliate links. It gives a clear view of how their promotions are performing.

## Visits

To check visits, the affiliate needs to log into their account and click on the **Visits** tab from the top menu of the user dashboard.

Once there, they will see a table with detailed information for each visit:

* **URL**: The URL that was clicked by the visitor.
* **Referring URL:** Displays the webpage URL where the affiliate link was clicked.
* **Converted**: Shows whether the visit led to a successful referral or not. If it is marked "Green Tick," the visitor completed a conversion. If it shows a "Warning Icon," the visitor didn't convert.
* **User Agent**: Displays the browser and device information used by the visitor.
* **Date**: The exact time and date when someone clicked the affiliate link.

This section gives affiliates a complete overview of their link activity and helps them understand which channels or content are performing best.
