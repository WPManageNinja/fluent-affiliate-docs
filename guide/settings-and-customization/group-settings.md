# Group Settings

FluentAffiliate lets you create affiliate groups with different commission rates. You can easily assign a group to any affiliate to apply the group's rate settings. This feature helps you manage different tiers of affiliates with varying commission structures.

## Creating Affiliate Groups

1. Go to your FluentAffiliate **Settings**
2. Select **Affiliate Groups** from the left sidebar
3. Click the **Add New Group** button

![Group Settings](/guide/public/images/settings-and-customization/group/group-settings-overview.webp)

### Group Configuration Options

When creating a new group, you'll need to fill in the following details:

* **Name**: Enter a name for your affiliate group
* **Rate Type**: Choose whether the commission will be a:
  * Flat amount
  * Percentage
* **Rate**: Set the commission value (percentage or flat rate per referral)
* **Status**: Select the group's status:
  * Active
  * Pending
  * Cancelled
  * Rejected
* **Note**: Add optional notes for affiliates

Once you've filled in all the details, click the **Create** button to save the group.

![Group Creation](/guide/public/images/settings-and-customization/group/create-new-group.webp)

## Managing Affiliate Groups

After creating a group, it will appear in the Affiliate Groups section with all its details.

### Editing or Deleting Groups

To manage an existing group:
1. Find the group in the list
2. Click the three-dot menu
3. Choose to either:
   * **Edit** the group settings
   * **Delete** the group

![Group Management](/guide/public/images/settings-and-customization/group/group-management.webp)

## Assigning Groups to Affiliates

When creating a new affiliate or editing an existing one:
1. Look for the **Affiliate Type** field in their profile
2. Select the desired group from the dropdown
3. The selected group's commission rate will automatically apply to that affiliate
