# General Settings

FluentAffiliate provides a central dashboard to manage your affiliates effectively. One of the key features is the ability to create and manage an affiliate portal where your affiliates can:
- Generate referral links
- Track their visits and payouts
- Access important information

This guide will show you how to configure the general settings for your affiliate portal.

## Accessing General Settings

1. Go to the **FluentAffiliate Dashboard**
2. Click on **Settings** in the navbar
3. Select **General Settings** from the left sidebar

## Available Options

### Affiliate Area
Select a page from your site where you want to set up your Affiliate Portal. This will be the main page where affiliates can:
- Log in to their accounts
- Generate affiliate links
- Track their performance
- Access their dashboard

### Terms and Conditions Page
Set up a dedicated page for your affiliate program's terms and conditions. This page should outline:
- Commission structures
- Payment terms
- Acceptable promotional methods
- Any restrictions or requirements
- Other important policies

### Terms and Conditions Label
Customize the label that appears on the affiliate registration checkbox. By default, it shows "I agree to the Terms and Conditions", but you can modify it to match your needs.

For example, if you set it to "Affiliate Rules", new affiliates will see "I agree to Affiliate Rules" during registration.

## Saving Your Settings

After configuring your settings, click the **Save Settings** button to apply your changes.
